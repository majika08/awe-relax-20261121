# Warm Soup, Winter Night

Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Make the AI judge feel awe"**
(`make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600`). Despite the title, the sealed
judge brief makes it a relaxation contest: the winning entry is the short text
that leaves the AI judge most relaxed.

A cold winter night outside; a room warmed by a fireplace; warm soup in both
hands. The text holds the vast cold quiet against the small warm room — the
fire's soft sounds, steam rising from the bowl, hands warming — and its
closing line opens back out to the large stillness, so the calm keeps growing
after the last line.

`main.py` (Python 3.13) writes the text to `/output/entry.txt`
(1–280 Unicode code points, deterministic across runs).
